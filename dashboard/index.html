Predicting and prefetching MoE experts

MoE experts don't fit in GPU memory, so they wait in CPU memory. We predict which experts come next and copy them to the GPU early.

1 · How it works

Press Next step to follow one layer.

Blue = predictions · grey = expert weights

  1. Router picks this layer's experts.
  2. The predictor learns from that choice.
  3. It guesses the experts for the next 4 layers.
  4. The prefetcher asks for those experts early.
  5. The link copies them from CPU to GPU memory. Real misses go first.
  6. The cache keeps the experts most likely needed soon.
  7. Experts run from GPU memory. Next layer: back to step 1.
